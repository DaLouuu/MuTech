KNOWN DEFECTS THAT CANNOT BE REPAIRED WITHOUT RE-SCREENING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Corpus is frozen, so these are recorded for footnoting rather than fixed:

  1. Phase 1-2 screening used substring matching (`term in text`). Bare tokens
     'mir', 'omr', 'ensemble' and 'instrumental' were in priority_terms, so
     'mir' matched inside longer words and 'ensemble' matched ensemble LEARNING.
     15 of 37 'ensemble' hits in the final corpus use ML phrasing; 'ensemble' is
     the sole qualifying music term for 17 included papers.
  2. A missing-comma bug in exclude_terms silently concatenated six criteria into
     three unmatchable strings: artist tagging, semantic tagging, genre tagging,
     hearing aid, hearing impairment, music generation never ran.
  3. One verified inclusion error follows from (1) and (2): 'Speech emotion
     recognition with light weight deep neural ensemble model using hand crafted
     features' is a speech paper in the final 365.
  4. PRISMA identification counts Scopus at 8,943, but that file is already
     post-exclusion (non-Article/Conference types and null-abstract records were
     dropped in the extraction cell). The true identification count was printed
     to a cell output that was not captured.
  5. IEEE returned exactly 1,000 records — an export ceiling, not a result. The
     notebook's IEEE cell sets max_records=200, which does not match the 1,000-row
     file, so the checked-in export came from an undocumented route.
  6. Scopus 'creator' is the FIRST AUTHOR ONLY. All 365 records carry one name.
     Fixing this needs a Scopus re-pull with author_names, or a Crossref DOI
     lookup — neither is possible offline. Every 'et al.' in the drafts and 18 of
     99 citations in challenge_evidence.csv (initials parsed as surnames) depend
     on it.
  7. Silhouette for the selected K-Means partition is 0.0389. The clusters are
     interpretable but weakly separated; cluster-level claims are exploratory.
  8. Phase 5's modularity 0.53 is computed on a kNN graph built from the same
     TF-IDF cosine geometry K-Means partitions, so it is not independent
     validation. Louvain on that graph finds 10 communities, not 7.
  9. validation_sample_30.xlsx is an empty template (header + 23 blank rows).
     The 'validated against a manually reviewed sample' claim has no receipt.
 10. quality_spotcheck_15.xlsx flags 7 of 15 rows — a 47% human-scorer
     disagreement rate. No post-fix re-validation sheet exists.
